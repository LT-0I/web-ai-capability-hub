Why Probe

Coffee as a lens for the world.

We believe the best cup of coffee asks as many questions as it answers. Probe is built on three principles.

01

Single Origin,
Traced to the Farm

Every bean we roast carries a passport — farm name, altitude, harvest date. We buy direct from producers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra so the story travels with the cup.

Our sourcing
02

Dialled with
Precision

Our baristas treat extraction like a discipline. Grind size, water temperature, contact time — each variable is documented and refined weekly. No guesswork, only method.

Our process
03

A Space Made
for Slow Time

No rush, no upsells. The Probe interior is designed for long reads, light conversations, and the kind of focused work that only happens somewhere genuinely calm.
