# Stream 4: Briefing

## Overview

Stream 4 is an exhaustive, interactive exploration of three leading consumer web AI platforms. The project subjects each system to a rigorous, hands-on testing regimen — moving well beyond surface-level prompting to probe the edges of reasoning, creativity, instruction-following, and real-world utility.

## Scope

Three consumer-facing AI assistants are evaluated side by side across a broad spectrum of tasks. Each platform is engaged interactively, with responses feeding back into subsequent prompts to simulate genuine, extended use rather than isolated snapshot testing.

## Methodology

Sessions are structured to stress-test each AI across core competencies:

- **Reasoning & logic** — multi-step problems, edge cases, and contradiction detection
- **Creative output** — long-form writing, ideation, and stylistic range
- **Instruction fidelity** — precision, constraint-following, and format adherence
- **Conversational depth** — coherence across extended, branching dialogues

## Goals

Stream 4 aims to produce an honest, comparative picture of where consumer AI currently stands — what each platform does well, where it stumbles, and how the three differ in character and capability.

## Audience

Researchers, enthusiasts, and practitioners seeking a ground-level, unfiltered account of the current consumer AI landscape.
